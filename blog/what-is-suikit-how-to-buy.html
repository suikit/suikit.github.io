What is SuiKit — and how to buy it

SuiKit is a source-available development kit for building trading bots on DeepBook V3, Sui's central limit order book. It is the hardened skeleton of a bot that has actually run on mainnet — sold as source code and documentation, not as a service, a signal feed, or a promise of profit.

What you get

  • Execution engine — the full BalanceManager lifecycle (free vs. locked balances), atomic cancel-and-place, MoveAbort code-3 mitigation, gas-reserve logic.
  • Decision layer — a deterministic 5-phase quant engine. An optional DeepSeek overlay exists, but no LLM ever touches the order book.
  • Ops tooling — live HTML dashboards, Telegram alerts, fill and cost-basis tracking, an error watcher, and stagnation monitoring out of the box.

Everything arrives as a private GitHub repository you clone and read. A good way to use it: point an AI coding agent at the repo — it starts from working answers instead of guesses.

What it is not

  • Not a managed service — you run it yourself.
  • Not a signal group — no calls, no copy-trading.
  • No profit guarantee — the strategies are reference implementations for study.
  • Not an official product — SuiKit is independent and not affiliated with Mysten Labs, the Sui Foundation, or DeepBook.

How to buy — step by step

  • 1. Open the checkout — click “Connect Wallet & Pay” on the landing page.
  • 2. Connect a Sui wallet — Slush, Sui Wallet, Suiet, or any wallet-standard Sui wallet. On mobile, open the page inside your wallet app's built-in browser.
  • 3. Pay 100 USDC (Sui mainnet) — one signature, one USDC transfer. Gas is paid in SUI by your wallet as usual. That's the only thing we will ever ask you to sign.
  • 4. Open the Telegram bot — the payment screen links to @sui_kit_bot with your transaction digest attached.
  • 5. Send your GitHub username — the bot verifies the digest on-chain, then invites you to the private repo. It usually takes under a minute. Adding your email is optional — it gets you priority invites to extended editions.

Paying from a wallet you can't connect? Send 100 USDC to the seller address shown in the checkout from any wallet or exchange, then send the transaction digest to the bot the same way. Verification is on-chain — it doesn't matter which wallet paid.

Early access is $100 USDC; the regular price will be $199. One payment, lifetime repository access.
