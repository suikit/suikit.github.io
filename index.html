Inside the repo

The edge cases are the product.

01

Balance lifecycle, done right

Track free vs locked vs settled balances per pool, sync them against on-chain state, and reconcile with open orders — so your bot never orders against money it doesn't have.

02

MoveAbort code 3 workaround

Automatically detect insufficient or locked DEEP fee balance and flip pay_with_deep=false — the single most common silent failure when placing DeepBook V3 orders.

03

Atomic order management

Cancel and place in one transaction. Order TTL re-extension on a 7-day cycle. Stale-price filtering. Satellite pool cleanup. All wired, all commented.

04

gRPC-native client

Direct Sui gRPC connectivity with a compatibility layer that mirrors the old JSON-RPC surface — no deprecated endpoints, no rewrites.

05

Fill & cost-basis tracking

Detect fills, maintain cost basis, persist trailing state and hold-benchmark tracking across restarts — the bookkeeping every strategy needs and nobody wants to write.

06

Safety by default

When price data is missing the bot keeps existing limits and does nothing. Duplicate-instance lock, cooldowns, daily caps, and consecutive-loss brakes are built in.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does this bot make money?

No claims are made — and none should be. This kit sells execution infrastructure and implementation know-how for DeepBook V3. The bundled strategies are reference implementations for study and extension, not a promise of profit. Trading digital assets involves real risk of loss.

What do I need to run it?

Node.js 20+, a Sui wallet with a DeepBook V3 BalanceManager object, and a small amount of SUI for gas. A DeepSeek API key and Ollama are optional — the deterministic quant engine runs without any LLM.

How is access delivered?

Connect a Sui wallet (Slush, Sui Wallet, etc.), pay $100 USDC on-chain, then send the transaction digest and your GitHub username via the link shown after payment. Once the payment is verified on-chain, you'll be invited to the private repository — with read access to main, including all documentation and future commits.

Delivery is instant: our Telegram bot verifies the digest on-chain and sends your GitHub invite within seconds.

Do early buyers get anything extra?

Yes. Buyers who register their email address (optional — just include it with your digest + GitHub username) get priority invitations and early-access offers when extended editions of the kit are released. Registration is also how you'll hear about major updates pushed to the repo.

Which pools does it trade?

The execution layer is pool-agnostic. The reference config concentrates on SUI/USDC for depth and treats satellite pools (DEEP, WAL, USDSUI, NS) as cleanup targets — a pattern you can extend.

What is the refund policy?

Payments are made on-chain in USDC and cannot be reversed. As this is digital source access delivered in full, all sales are final — no refunds once the payment is verified. The only exception is a fault on our side (e.g., payment verified but the repo invite was never delivered). If in doubt, please ask before buying.

Can I redistribute or resell it?

No. The license grants personal use only — use, read, and modify for yourself. Public redistribution and resale are prohibited. See the LICENSE file in the repo.

Is it production-tested?

The codebase is a snapshot of a bot that has run live on Sui mainnet. That said, your environment differs — the README is explicit that live operation is your responsibility. Start small, watch the dashboard, and keep the kill-switch script handy.

Which network and asset do you accept?

USDC on Sui mainnet only. The checkout signs a single transaction that transfers exactly $100 USDC from your wallet to the seller's Sui address — no other chains or tokens are supported.

Why a one-time payment, not a subscription?

Because we'd rather reach more builders at a lower price. A single $100 payment keeps the kit accessible to individuals, and it includes every future commit on main — no monthly drip, no tier gates.
